About MILUN

MILUN Studio is the paper practice of an architect based in Paris. It began in 2025 with CRIATURA and HUACA, small paper sculptures cut and folded by hand, one by one.

In 2026 the studio started designing pop-ups with its own software, so that anyone can build these small architectures at home. Every template is tested in paper before it is published.
